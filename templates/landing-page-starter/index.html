Quick answers

Before you start.

Practical answers about how Brand works and how you move forward with Partner Cebu.

What is Brand Clean Energy?

Brand Clean Energy is a local solar marketing and referral brand operated by [Your Company]. We help Cebu homeowners and businesses understand their options and connect them with Partner Cebu for the technical work.

Is Brand the installer?

No. Brand does not install solar systems. Partner Cebu performs the technical assessment, system design, installation, and warranty service. Brand connects you to them.

Who issues the quotation and system design?

Partner Cebu. After their technical site assessment, Partner Cebu prepares the final system design, capacity, and written quotation.

Who receives my payment?

Payment arrangements are handled directly with Partner Cebu under their terms. Brand does not collect payment for solar systems.

Who provides the warranty?

Warranty and after-sales service are provided by Partner Cebu together with the equipment manufacturers, based on the terms in your final written quotation.

Can Brand help me choose a package?

Brand can share general information to help you get oriented, then connect you with Partner Cebu. Partner Cebu confirms the right system for your home after their assessment.

Does Brand charge me for the referral?

No. Inquiring through Brand is free. You simply tell us your needs and we connect you with Partner Cebu.

What is [Your Company]?

[Your Company] is the operator behind Brand Clean Energy. [Your Company] builds and runs the marketing and referral brand that connects customers with Partner Cebu.

Is the result on this page a final recommendation?

No. It is general information only. The final system design, capacity, and price require a technical site assessment by Partner Cebu.

Are the assessments free?

Yes. Inquiring through Brand is free, and Partner Cebu's site assessment is free within confirmed service coverage. Inter-island visits are scheduled after location and transport access are confirmed.

What happens after I send my inquiry?

You will see general package information, then continue to Messenger to send your inquiry summary. Brand reviews it and connects you with Partner Cebu for the next step.

How is the right package size determined?

Partner Cebu reviews your electricity use, daytime loads, backup priorities, roof or site conditions, and budget during their technical assessment. The packages shown here are general starting points, not a technical recommendation.

Will hybrid solar still work during a brownout?

It can power the backup circuits selected in the final design, within the inverter and battery limits. It does not automatically mean every appliance in the house will run during an outage.

How long can the battery power my home?

Runtime depends on usable battery capacity and the appliances running. Air conditioners, heaters, pumps, and other high-power loads use stored energy faster, so Partner Cebu calculates this during their assessment.

Do solar panels work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes, but output is usually lower than on a clear day. Actual production varies with sunlight, shading, panel placement, weather, and system design.

Is every roof suitable for solar panels?

Not always. Partner Cebu checks usable space, shading, roof condition, structure, orientation, and safe access before confirming an installation plan.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on system size, roof conditions, electrical work, weather, site access, material availability, and any required approvals. Partner Cebu confirms your schedule after the site assessment.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Typical care includes monitoring performance, inspecting the equipment, and cleaning panels when dirt affects production. Partner Cebu advises the recommended frequency based on your location and site conditions.

Can I pay by credit card or in-house financing?

These options may be available through Partner Cebu. Financing remains subject to eligibility, required documents, approval, and the final written terms. Card availability and payment terms are confirmed by Partner Cebu before payment.

What warranty applies to my system?

Warranty coverage depends on the selected equipment and the terms stated in your final quotation from Partner Cebu. The applicable product, workmanship, and support coverage are explained before you proceed.

Why don’t you ask for my bill image?

We only collect what is needed for the first inquiry. Partner Cebu can request a bill privately later. Cover account numbers before sharing it.
